NEEDS SYSTEM

Your operative tracks Hunger and Warmth (each 0–5). Certain nodes and actions drain these. Hitting 0 Hunger while it's being drained deals -3 HP per instance. Hitting 0 Warmth reduces DEF by 1. Both can be restored via items or specific story nodes.

SAVE SYSTEM

Saves are stored in browser localStorage per slot and can be exported as JSON strings — useful for moving between devices or importing into Underpass 2. Use [ COPY SAVE ] in the header to access export and slot management.

// COMBAT TIPS
  • Use HEAVY when you need to break through high DEF — d8 roll significantly outperforms d6 at the cost of a 10% miss chance.

STORY

You wake under Highway 9 with no memory and a note in your sleeve addressed from yourself: Find Mara — Tunnel 9. She has what they want. Helios Corporation's data shard — containing evidence of an illegal population trial run on Sector 9's residents — is hidden in their factory. Mara, a former Helios data architect, needs an unknown operative to retrieve it.

The story unfolds across five zones, culminating in a confrontation with Nakamura — Helios' field commander — on the Sector 7 bridge.

ENDINGS

STANDARD ENDING

Defeat Nakamura and cross the bridge. Mara confirms the data is transmitting on six feeds. You walk. The city glows behind you.

MARA — COFFEE ENDING (REL ≥ 3)

After the standard ending, a terminal message from Mara: "I owe you a proper coffee. Above ground, somewhere." A quiet acknowledgement without commitment.

MARA — ROMANCE ENDING (REL ≥ 4 + confession flag)

The fullest ending. Mara's message: "Data is live. Six feeds. Unstoppable." Then — she found a place above ground. Morning light. She'll send coordinates. "Come find me."

// ENDING REQUIREMENTS
  • Romance ending requires: REL 4 + mara_confession flag. To reach this: at REL 3, choose "I want to know more about you" (+1 to REL 4), then "Tell her she matters to you" (confession, sets flag, gives flask).
  • After confession, the NPC panel refreshes showing the REL 4 options. Choose "I'll come back" to head to the factory — NOT the bridge.
↑

TIPS & STRATEGY

// OPTIMAL EARLY GAME PATH
  • Search the underpass first (utility knife + medkit + ¢28) before heading to the tunnel entrance.
  • Talk your way past the ganger ("Looking for Mara") — saves HP and earns ¢20. Fighting him early with only the knife is risky.
  • In the tunnel network, speak to Gray before Mara — the Nakamura intel is free and permanently unlocks a bridge approach option.
  • Check the maintenance hatch if you have HP to spare — the hound drops ¢25 + power cell.
// FACTORY — ROUTE CHOICE
  • The service corridor (Wire Cutters required) is the best route — bypasses two guard fights and the ghost operative, reaching the server room with full HP.
  • The ghost operative (ATK 10) is the hardest pre-boss fight. If going direct, use stims or adrenaline beforehand.
  • The EMP Scrambler (from Mara's intel dialogue) skips the enforcer fight entirely — worth getting even if you plan to fight through everything else.
// NAKAMURA FIGHT PREP
  • Target HP going into the bridge: 30+ ideally. Stock at least one medkit or power cell.
  • Mara's Flask (+20 HP + permanent +1 ATK) is extremely strong here if saved — pop it at the start of the fight or when HP drops low.
  • Ghost Chip provides guaranteed evade for 2 turns — use it immediately when Nakamura enters rage mode (≤25 HP).
  • Gray's intel unlocks bridge_gray which gives a slight tactical edge in the opening — worth having.

SCAVENGE SYSTEM

The transit hub maintenance bay introduces a scavenge event early. Each depth level (1–4) triggers a random event — loot, gold, or nothing. Going deeper costs 1 Hunger. Events include: medkit, power cell, ¢35–60 cash, combat blade, neural jack, or scavenge stims.

COMBAT GUIDE

Combat uses the same engine as Underpass 1. Key differences in U2: enemies are generally tougher from the start, and NAKAMURA-Ω is a substantially harder final boss.

// NAKAMURA-Ω — FINAL BOSS
  • 120 HP, ATK 18, DEF 8 — significantly harder than U1 Nakamura. At 60 HP remaining, rage mode activates (ATK ×1.35).
  • Cannot bleed — fully synthetic. EMP Scrambler stuns him (he's augmented).
  • Recommended approach: reach the Spire at level 3+, with Hardplate or Body Armor equipped, and at least two healing consumables.
  • The Overclock Chip (+8 ATK, -5 HP recoil) is excellent here — high damage output compensates for his heavy DEF.

TIPS & STRATEGY

// RECOMMENDED PATH
  • Go to the market district before Mara — the intel node gives you the Biometric Cloak for free, which lets you bypass Spire lobby security entirely.
  • On the server floor (optional), fight the Spire Elite for the Overclock Chip — it's an exceptional consumable for the NAKAMURA-Ω fight.
  • Take the legacy terminal detour in B2 — it has no cost and unlocks the easter egg. The Revenant Guard fight is mandatory anyway.
// NAKAMURA-Ω PREPARATION
  • Target stats going into the Spire: HP 55+, DEF 6+ (Hardplate strongly recommended at ¢140). The boss hits for 18 ATK base.
  • Stock: Overclock Chip + at least 2 medkits. Pop the Overclock Chip at round 1 — the +8 ATK for 4 turns dramatically shortens a fight where you do not want to overstay.
  • At 60 HP his ATK goes to ~24. This is when to use the Ghost Chip if you have one.
  • EMP Scrambler stuns him for one turn — use it in rage phase to buy time.
// U1 IMPORT STRATEGY
  • A level 7 U1 veteran starts with HP 70, ATK 10, DEF 4 — substantial. The Spire Elite and Revenant Guard become manageable fights rather than threatening ones.
  • Carry Mara's Flask from U1 — it heals 8 HP in U2, a modest but reliable consumable in early game before you stock up.
  • The imported Data Shard is cosmetic in U2 (key item, no mechanical use) but carries narrative weight.
